Sales policy

All sales are final

These are digital products. The moment you buy, you have the full source code and it cannot be returned. For that reason every sale is final, and there is no change-of-mind refund period.

By completing a purchase you agree to immediate delivery and, where your local law allows you to waive it, you give up any statutory cooling-off period for digital content.

So ask first

This policy puts the burden on me to describe things accurately and on you to check before buying. Both are easy:

  • Every product page lists exactly what the tool produces and what it needs to run.
  • Every tool ships a doctor command that reports what your machine can actually do — the answer to most "will this work for me" questions.
  • Email supul9229kalhara@gmail.com before you buy and I will tell you honestly whether a tool fits your case, including when the answer is no.

The one exception

If a product does not do what its page says it does, that is a defect, not a change of mind. Tell me and I will fix it, or refund you if I cannot. This applies however long ago you bought it.

Setup problems are usually not defects — a missing dependency or a model server that is not running is fixable, and I will help you fix it. Send the output of the tool's doctor command and I will normally have you working within a day.

What this policy cannot override

Two things sit above it, and I would rather say so than pretend otherwise:

  • Your statutory rights. Some countries grant consumers rights that cannot be waived by a policy like this one. Where that applies to you, those rights win.
  • The payment platform's own rules. Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad act as merchant of record on their sales and operate their own buyer-protection policies. They can issue a refund independently of anything stated here.

Chargebacks

If something is wrong, email me. A chargeback costs us both more than a conversation does, and I have never refused to sort out a genuine problem.
