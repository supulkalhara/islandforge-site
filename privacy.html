Privacy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This website

This site is static, hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and builds no profile of you. GitHub records server logs including IP addresses as part of serving the page; that is their processing, described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. If that matters to you, a content blocker will stop it and the site will still work.

When you buy something

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad. They collect what they need to take payment and meet tax obligations, under their own privacy policies. I never see your full card details. What reaches me is your email address, what you bought, and the country used for tax — enough to deliver the product and answer support questions.

When you email me

Your message and address sit in my mailbox. I use them to answer you and to keep a record of what was agreed. I do not add you to a mailing list, and there is no newsletter.

What the software does

The tools run entirely on your machine. They contain no telemetry, no analytics and no licence phone-home. They talk only to endpoints you configure yourself — a local model server, or a service whose API key you supplied. Your manuscripts, designs, prompts and output never reach Island Forge.

Keeping and deleting

Purchase records are kept as long as tax rules require. Support email is kept while it is useful and deleted when it is not. To see what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email supul9229kalhara@gmail.com and I will action it within thirty days.

Children

These are professional developer tools, not intended for or marketed to children.
